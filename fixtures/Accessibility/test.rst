Accessibility
#############

The user suffers from a hearing loss.

Readers suffering from low vision can enlarge the text.

The device is not suitable for disabled people.

A person who is crippled cannot reach the button.

The app supports hearing-impaired and visually impaired users.

Do not label someone as a mute person.

Readers who are blind or have low vision use screen readers.

The CMSE disabled build runs in the secure processing environment.

Press the Mute button to mute the speaker.

The ``disabled`` property and the disabled state are set in the UI.

Call :c:func:`suffers_from` to read the state.
